One thing becomes clear rather quickly.

Whether it's software, an idea, a perspective, or a better version of himself, Dhruv is rarely standing still. Building, for him, has never been about recognition—it's simply how he chooses to move through life.

It doesn't take many conversations to realize this.

He isn't searching for certainty; he's searching for meaning. The conversations he values, the books he reads, and the questions he asks all reflect a quiet desire to understand the world—and himself—a little more deeply.
